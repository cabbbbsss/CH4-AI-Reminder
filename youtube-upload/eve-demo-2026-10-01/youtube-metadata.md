## Description

small things are easy to forget, but a fixed reminder often shows up at the wrong time.

in this demo, we show EVE, an iPhone app our team built at Apple Developer Academy. EVE uses calendar context, saved places, and lightweight feedback to help reminders arrive at a more useful moment.

we walk through the onboarding flow, the adaptive reminder experience, location-based reminders, and the insights EVE keeps on device. the AI features use Apple's on-device Foundation Models, while calendar events, locations, learned routines, and reminder history stay on the device.

EVE is designed around small reminders like keys, earphones, chargers, or medication. the AI experience needs a compatible iPhone with Apple Intelligence enabled. the current app is English-only, and its EVE Plus purchase flow uses RevenueCat Test Store rather than App Store purchases.

EVE was built by Caca, Nanda, Dani, Amanda, and Keiko at Apple Developer Academy.
